    How we scored

    Each ad is rendered to a 1080×1080 PNG in a headless browser and measured by tools/score-ads.js. Same input, same score.

    Score = 35% colour + 25% contrast + 25% CTA + 15% detail (0–100).

    • Colour: Hasler–Süsstrunk colourfulness M = σrgyb + 0.3·μrgyb, capped at 100.
    • Contrast: WCAG contrast ratio between the 5th and 95th percentile luminance, (ratio−1)/20.
    • CTA: half WCAG contrast of the "Shop now" colour vs the dominant background ((ratio−1)/6, capped), half its area share (4% = full).
    • Detail: edge density (luminance gradient > 0.08); 12% is ideal, less or more scores lower.
